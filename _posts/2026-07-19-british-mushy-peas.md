A traditional British side dish made from marrowfat peas. I needed a side when I made [toad in the hole]({% post_url "2025-03-29-toad-in-the-hole" %}), and this was the perfect accompaniment. Mushy peas are often served with fish and chips, but they also pair well with other British dishes.

### Ingredients

- 12 ounces dried marrowfat peas
- boiling water
- 1 tablespoon baking soda
- 4 cups water
- 1/2 tablespoon salt

### Directions

1. Dissolve baking soda in boiling water and pour over peas in a bowl, covering them by about 3 inches. Stir and soak for at least 12 hours.
1. Drain the peas and rinse. Place in a saucepan with 4 cups of water and bring to a boil. Cover and reduce heat to medium-low, and simmer for half-an-hour, stirring occasionally.
1. When peas have completely broken down, add salt and stir. Adjust consistency by continuing to simmer or adding water as needed.
1. If desired, splash in a little cream, butter, and/or malt vinegar for extra flavor. Serve warm as a side dish.
